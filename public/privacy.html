Privacy

A lawyer still has to write the real policy before this site takes public accounts. The notes below describe what the software stores today so that review has a starting point.

Account

An account stores an email address, a display name, and a password hash. The session cookie is HttpOnly. A separate cookie keeps a CSRF token.

Games and plays

Published games store the title, description, mechanic, and, for sandbox games, the HTML document. A play start stores a hash of the session and IP address, not the raw IP. That hash is used to count unique players for Trending and to ignore bursts. The creator's own signed-in plays are left out of that rank.

Reports and moderation

Abuse reports store a reason, optional details, and the reporter when they are signed in. Moderators are the addresses in ADMIN_EMAILS. Hide, unhide, archive, feature, ban, and report decisions are written to an audit log.

What this draft does not do

It does not name a controller, a retention period, a contact address, or a region. Do not link to it as if those promises exist.
